# Insurance

All 3 products in this package.

Every entry below is taken from the package's product inventory. Nothing here is a plan or a
roadmap item; all of it is built.

---

## Devadex Insurance OS

112 files, 23 tests

The agency management platform: book of business, clients, policies, claims, coverages, quotes, payments, documents, a renewals pipeline, endorsements, commissions and tasks.

## Devadex Broker

31 files, 11 tests

Micro-brokerage rating tool: rates general liability, tools and equipment, commercial auto, workers' compensation and umbrella cover with an explainable engine, binds one-year policies into a book, and issues certificates.

## Workers' Comp Optimizer

9 files, 94 tests

An autonomous premium auditor: checks every employee class code, recalculates the experience modifier, and finds overcharges.
